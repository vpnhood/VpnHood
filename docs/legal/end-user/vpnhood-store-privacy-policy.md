# VpnHood! STORE - Privacy Policy

*Effective: 2026-11-17.*

**OmegaHood LLC** ("we", "us", or "our") is the controller of the data described in this policy. This policy applies to **VpnHood! STORE**: the hosted service for making and running a store, and its pages at **store.vpnhood.com**. It is for the people who use it: whoever owns a store, and whoever works in a store's team.

A store's own customers are not covered by this policy. Each store decides how its customers' data is used and has its own privacy policy for them; we process that data only on the store's behalf, as the VpnHood! STORE [Terms of Use](https://www.vpnhood.com/legal/vpnhood-store/terms-of-use/) set out.

We never sell your data, and VpnHood! STORE shows no ads.

## What We Collect

- **Your account** — you sign in with your VpnHood ID, which tells us the identifier of your account there, your email address, whether it has been confirmed, and your name. We keep them, with the time your account here was made. We never get your password. The VpnHood ID has its own [Privacy Policy](https://www.vpnhood.com/legal/vpnhood-id/privacy-policy/).
- **Your stores** — the stores you own or work in, what you are in each and when you first came to it, and what you put there, such as a store's name. A store's keys to outside services are kept encrypted and are never shown again.
- **Someone adds you to a store's team** — they type your email address, and we keep it so that you get into that store when you sign in with it. Until you come to that store, its team sees only the address they typed.
- **A record of what was done** — who made or changed a store, its team, its owner or its keys, and when, including the version of the terms that a store's owner accepted. It holds no IP address, and never a key.
- **Your device and connection** — your IP address and the details of each request, such as the page and the time, are used for a short while to deliver the pages and to protect accounts and stores. Our service does not store them with your account.

## Cookies

VpnHood! STORE sets no cookies of its own. It keeps your sign-in in the storage of your browser tab, which is cleared when you close the tab or sign out. It has no analytics, no advertising, and no scripts or pictures from other websites. The VpnHood ID, where you sign in, uses the cookies its own policy describes.

## How We Use Your Data

- To sign you in and show you your stores.
- To let a store's team work together: the people in a store see who else is in it, and as what.
- To approve stores, and to write to a store's owner about their store when we need to. We send no newsletters or advertising from VpnHood! STORE.
- To keep the record of what was done in a store, for its people and for us.
- To protect accounts, stores and the service from abuse.

## Who Receives Your Data

- **The people of your stores.** The team of a store you are in sees your email address and what you are there, and your name once you have come to that store.
- **Service providers** that host our servers and database, deliver and protect the pages, and keep our encrypted backups. They process the data only for the purposes described above. A provider that delivers the pages may also keep its own records of requests, under its own policy.

## Security

Every page and connection is encrypted. Each store's data is kept apart from every other store's, a store's keys are encrypted with keys of ours that are not kept with them, and the record of what was done can't be changed afterwards.

## How Long We Keep Data

We keep your account until you ask us to delete it. A draft store that its owner deletes loses its team and its keys at once; its name and the record of what was done in it are kept. When an account is deleted, the record of what was done keeps only an identifier that no longer leads to you. Deleted data stays in our encrypted backups for up to 14 days.

## Delete Your Account

To delete your account at VpnHood! STORE, write to us from its email address (see **Contact Us** below). If you own a store, hand it to someone in its team or delete it first, or tell us what should become of it. Deleting your account here does not delete your VpnHood ID, which its own policy covers.

If a store added your email address to its team and you don't want that, someone in that team can take it out, and so can you once you have signed in; or write to us.

## Why We Process Data

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on:

- **contract**, to provide the Service you ask for: your account, your stores and their teams; and
- **legitimate interests** in keeping a record of what was done in a store, securing accounts and the service, preventing abuse, and answering the messages you send us.

## International Transfers

We are based in the United States, and our providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, transfers from the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or Switzerland use a recognized safeguard, such as an adequacy decision or approved standard contractual clauses.

## Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may ask to access, correct, delete, restrict, or receive a copy of your personal data, or object to certain processing. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority. To exercise a right, contact us below; we may need to verify your request.

## Children's Privacy

VpnHood! STORE is not directed to anyone under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we learn that a minor has given us personal information, we delete it.
