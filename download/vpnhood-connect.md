The free plan is only in the Android app from Google Play. It is funded by ads, so it is not available in China, Russia
or other countries where ads are not served. The other versions, the Android app from GitHub included, have no free
servers: they need [Premium](https://www.vpnhood.com/free-vpn/go-premium/), which has no ads.

## Android and Android TV

Android 7.0 or later, on phones, tablets and Android TV. [Set it up on your TV](https://www.vpnhood.com/features/tv/).

Get it from Google Play when you can. The APK from GitHub does not use Google Play. It is the ARM64 version, for most
phones; for old phones and many Android TVs, download the
[universal version](https://github.com/vpnhood/VpnHood.App.Connect/releases/latest/download/VpnHoodConnect-android-web.apk).

## iPhone, iPad and Apple TV

iOS or iPadOS 18.0 or later, and tvOS 17.0 or later on Apple TV.
[Set it up on your TV](https://www.vpnhood.com/features/tv/).

## Windows

Windows 10 or later, x64. The app needs a processor with SSE4.2 and POPCNT, as every processor of the last ten years
has.

## Linux

Run this command, on x64 or ARM64:

```bash
sudo bash -c 'u=https://github.com/vpnhood/VpnHood.App.Connect/releases/latest/download/VpnHoodConnect-linux.sh; bash <(wget -qO- $u 2>/dev/null || curl -fsSL $u)'
```

It supports Ubuntu 22.04 and later (Kubuntu and Lubuntu too), Debian 13 and Arch Linux; other distributions with systemd
should also work. The website has the rest: [install it on Linux](https://www.vpnhood.com/free-vpn/download/linux/).
